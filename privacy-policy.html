Cookies

We may use cookies and other technologies or methods of web and mobile analysis (e.g., Pixels, Beacons etc.) to gather, store, and track certain information related with your access to and activity through the Services. A "cookie" is a small piece of information that a website assign to your device while you are viewing a website. Cookies are very helpful and may be used for various different purposes. These purposes include, among other things, allowing you to navigate between pages efficiently, enabling automatic activation of certain features, remembering your preferences and making the interaction between you and the Services quicker, easier and smoother.

When you first visit our website, a cookie banner lets you choose which non-essential cookies you allow. We group cookies into the following categories:

  • Essential cookies which are required for the website to function and cannot be switched off. These include cookies used by our live chat widget (Chatwoot) so you can start and continue a conversation with our team, and by our scheduling provider (Calendly) on the "Book a Demo" page so you can book a call.
  • Analytics cookies which help us understand how visitors use the Services (e.g., via Google Tag Manager) so we can improve them. These are only set if you accept them.
  • Advertising cookies which are used to personalize ads and measure their performance across our advertising partners. These are only set if you accept them.

You can accept, decline, or customize non-essential cookies at any time by clicking "Cookie Settings" in the website footer. You may also remove cookies through your browser's own settings; however, if you disable cookies, some features of the Services (including live chat and demo scheduling) may not operate properly and your online experience may be limited.
