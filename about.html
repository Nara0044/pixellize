About NudgeBee

The AIOps platform for AI SRE, Cloud Cost Optimization and CloudOps

NudgeBee is an AIOps platform that brings AI SRE, Cloud Cost Optimization, and Kubernetes and Cloud Operations workflows together in one place. Instead of switching between an incident tool, a cost dashboard, and a runbook system, teams run day-2 cloud operations from a single platform: AI assistants investigate incidents to a cited root cause, right-size cloud spend automatically, and keep Kubernetes and multi-cloud environments healthy, plus a no-code automation builder for everything specific to your environment. The source is available to read, and you can self-host it in your own cluster and run it in production internally for free.

What we do

Most teams have plenty of dashboards and alerts. What they lack is a system that acts on what those signals mean. NudgeBee closes that gap: it reasons over your existing observability, cloud, and Kubernetes systems in place - no data ingestion required - and executes fixes through pull requests, approval-gated runbooks, and automations, with a human in the loop at every step.

Leadership

NudgeBee was founded in 2024 by Rakesh Rajendran and Shiv Pratap Singh. Both previously worked at Saama, a Bay Area data and analytics company, where they watched Day-2 cloud operations grow into one of the hardest unsolved problems in enterprise engineering - and set out to build the platform that closes that gap.
